# Characters — The Kids of Hollybrook

Status: **first roster draft**. Names, ages and abilities are proposals.

## Squad structure

- A crew is **four kids**. A roster of **12 kids at launch**, more as updates.
- Each kid has one **role**, a **passive**, two **actives** and a
  **Snow Day Special** (ultimate, charged by hits, builds and revives).
- Abilities come from a real hobby or personality, then exaggerated. No magic,
  no superpowers — everything is a kid's idea of what's possible.

### Roles

| Role | Job | Icon |
|---|---|---|
| **Thrower** | Soaking damage at range | Snowball |
| **Builder** | Makes and repairs cover | Bucket |
| **Scout** | Speed, flanking, information | Binoculars |
| **Heavy** | Pushes, takes hits, big snowballs | Boulder |
| **Support** | Warmth and revives | Thermos |
| **Trickster** | Decoys, disguise, mind-games | Snowman hat |

### Stats (1–5)

- **Arm** — throw range and speed.
- **Aim** — how tight the throw arc's landing spread is.
- **Hustle** — movement speed.
- **Grit** — size of the Warmth meter.
- **Craft** — packing and building speed.

## The Maple Court Crew (the player's starting four)

### Maisie "Fastball" Okafor — Thrower, age 10

Little League's best pitcher, fifth grade. Bossy, brilliant, keeps score out
loud. Wears her dad's oversized batting gloves over her mittens.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 |

- **Passive — Wind-up:** throws after moving are faster.
- **Fastball:** a flat, very fast throw that ignores wind.
- **Curveball:** a throw that curves around the edge of cover.
- **Special — Strikeout:** three rapid throws at three targets.
- **Bark:** "Strike three!"

### Bernard "Bunker" Lindqvist — Builder, age 11

Has a clipboard. Has a plan. Has, in fact, *several* plans, and all of them
involve walls. Wears a bright orange bobble hat and a high-vis vest borrowed
from his mom's work.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 5 |

- **Passive — Foreman:** allies building next to him build 50% faster.
- **Brick Mould:** places a snow wall segment instantly (uses a bucket).
- **Patch-up:** fully repairs damaged cover in a small radius.
- **Special — The Fortress:** raises a ring of walls around him.
- **Bark:** "That's load-bearing!"

### Pip Tanaka — Scout, age 7

The youngest kid on the street and everyone's little brother or sister. Tiny,
fearless and impossible to catch. Bright yellow one-piece snowsuit; mittens on
a string.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 | 5 | 2 | 3 |

- **Passive — Too Small to Hit:** smaller hitbox; lobs often go over.
- **Belly Slide:** a fast slide forward on snow or ice, passes under arcs.
- **Snow Angel:** lies down and becomes nearly invisible in fresh snow.
- **Special — "I'm Telling!":** shouts — every enemy is revealed and flinches
  (interrupts packing and building).
- **Bark:** "You can't get me!"

### Rosa Delgado — Support, age 9

Brings a flask of hot chocolate to everything. Kind, organised, quietly
competitive. A red toggle parka and a huge knitted scarf her abuela made.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |

- **Passive — Spare Mittens:** allies nearby lose Warmth more slowly.
- **Cocoa:** restores Warmth to an ally (a pour animation, a little steam).
- **Run Them Home:** brings a Soaked ally back from the bench early.
- **Special — Cocoa Break:** every ally in a wide circle is fully warmed and
  briefly can't be chilled.
- **Bark:** "Drink it while it's hot!"

## Recruitable kids (beaten crews' captains and members)

### Tobias "Tank" Brennan — Heavy, age 12

The biggest kid on Hill Road, gentle until the snowballs start. Striped football
jersey over a hoodie, no hat, no matter the weather.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 5 | 3 |

- **Passive — Thick Skin:** the first hit each round does nothing.
- **Roll One Up:** pushes a boulder that grows as it rolls and stops as cover.
- **Shoulder Charge:** barges through a snow wall.
- **Special — Avalanche:** a giant boulder down a lane; flattens cover, soaks.
- **Bark:** "Comin' through!"

### Ada Voss — Thrower (Inventor), age 11

A tinkerer with goggles on her bobble hat. Her gadgets are made of hockey
sticks, buckets and gaffer tape, and only work about 80% of the time.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 4 |

- **Passive — Spare Parts:** carries one extra snowball.
- **Bucket Catapult:** places a launcher that lobs snowballs automatically.
- **Snow Cannon:** a heavy, long-range lob (after a short wind-up).
- **Special — SnowBlaster 3000:** a leaf blower full of powder; a cone that
  pushes and blinds. Has a small chance to sputter (comic, not punishing).
- **Bark:** "It's supposed to do that."

### Felix Moreau — Trickster, age 10

Class clown, wants to be a magician. Always has a carrot in his pocket.
Striped hat with a pom-pom bigger than his head.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 |

- **Passive — Misdirection:** enemies' aim spread is wider against him.
- **Decoy Snowman:** a quick snowman that draws throws and soaks them up.
- **Hat Swap:** swaps places with any snowman on the map.
- **Special — Snowman Army:** five decoy snowmen; one of them is Felix.
- **Bark:** "Ta-daaa!"

### Jun Park — Scout (Lookout), age 12

Quiet, birdwatcher, climbs anything. Binoculars round her neck and a green
parka with a fur-trim hood.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 1 |

- **Passive — Eagle Eye:** sees further; reveals footprints.
- **Climb:** gets up into a tree for height, range and sight.
- **Shake the Branches:** dumps snow on everyone under a nearby tree.
- **Special — Spotted:** marks enemies for the whole crew; marked targets take
  extra chill.
- **Bark:** "Got eyes on 'em."

### Wren Whitaker & Biscuit — Support (Dog), age 8

A farm kid who's never seen without Biscuit, a scruffy terrier in a tartan
coat. (Biscuit is the most popular character in every playtest. Guaranteed.)

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

- **Passive — Good Boy:** Biscuit fetches snowballs — Wren never runs out of
  ammo.
- **Fetch!:** Biscuit runs to a point and steals the enemy flag / scarf, or
  knocks a snowball out of an enemy's hands.
- **Zoomies:** Biscuit tears around in a circle; enemies inside are distracted.
- **Special — Walkies:** Biscuit drags Wren at high speed along a path,
  knocking down drifts.
- **Bark:** "Biscuit, NO! ...Good boy."

### Zara Kapoor — Scout (Skater), age 11

Figure skater, takes it very seriously. Lilac jacket, earmuffs, skates tied
over her shoulder until she finds ice.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 2 |

- **Passive — Edge Control:** full control on ice (no sliding), faster on it.
- **Glide:** a long dash that turns ground into a short ice trail.
- **Spin Throw:** a 360° throw that hits all adjacent enemies.
- **Special — Triple Axel:** leaps over cover and throws on landing.
- **Bark:** "And... stick the landing."

### Hamish McBride — Heavy (Goalie), age 10

Ice-hockey goalie, has all the pads. Insists he's Canadian. (He's from
Ohio.)

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 2 |

- **Passive — Pads:** frontal hits do half chill.
- **Glove Save:** catches one incoming snowball and keeps it.
- **Slapshot:** knocks an incoming snowball back where it came from (timed in
  real-time, a reaction order in WEGO).
- **Special — Brick Wall:** for a few seconds, blocks every throw in a wide arc
  in front of him.
- **Bark:** "Not in MY net."

### Ollie & Ellie Hart — Trickster (Twins), age 9

Identical twins in matching coats. Nobody can tell them apart, which they
exploit ruthlessly. Counts as one kid in the squad but plays as two small kids
sharing one Warmth meter.

| Arm | Aim | Hustle | Grit | Craft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 2 |

- **Passive — Which One?:** two bodies, one Warmth pool.
- **Switcheroo:** swap the twins' positions.
- **Pincer:** both throw at the same target from two sides (back-hit bonus).
- **Special — Double Trouble:** both twins act twice.
- **Bark:** "It was HIM." / "It was HER."

## Kid profile card (UI content)

Every kid has:

- Portrait (3 expressions: happy, determined, soaked)
- Name, nickname, age, street, role icon
- Five stat pips
- Likes / dislikes (e.g. Pip: *likes* — sledding, bananas; *dislikes* —
  bedtime, being called "little")
- Signature bark (voice line)
- Bio (2–3 lines)
- Unlockable trivia (earned through play)
- Cosmetic slots: hat, scarf (team colour), coat, mittens, sled
